### **1. Set Radical, Extreme Ambitions**

- **Aim to build the best company in the world:** Set aspirational, extreme goals—such as building the "greatest cathedral" rather than just a nice church—even if it seems nearly impossible. Emotional, material, and learning rewards compound exponentially at the extreme ends of commitment.
- **Reinvent from first principles:** Emulating tried-and-tested formulas from major hubs guarantees mediocrity at best. Thinking independently from first principles allows a company to invent unique operational methods that yield a distinct competitive advantage.

### **2. Prioritise Raw Talent Over Experience**

- **Capitalise on the 10x productivity gap:** In digital technology, the contribution gap between average employees and top performers is immense, with top talent delivering over 10 times the value.
- **Hire green, high-potential graduates:** Industry experience often gets stale quickly, normalises low performance standards, or instils political habits. Smart, eager individuals with strong baseline cognitive capacity can accumulate necessary experience rapidly while bringing immense drive.
- **Centralise hiring to fix incentive alignment:** Team managers often have poor hiring incentives—such as rushing to fill open seats to reduce workload or avoiding candidates who require coaching. Centralising recruitment under dedicated talent teams eliminates local bias, leverages larger data samples, and protects hiring standards.
- **Treat recruitment as a science:** Move away from intuitive interviews or resume track records by evaluating over 100 predictive signals (e.g., candidate behavior when interacting with administrative support staff) alongside standardized cognitive testing.

### **3. Instil Radical Cultural Tenets**

- **Enforce Extreme Ownership:** Select exclusively for individuals who care intensely about excelling, mastering their craft, and driving organizational success. Maintaining high talent density around this mindset creates a self-reinforcing, contagious momentum across teams.
- **Practice Relentless Simplification:** System complexity grows non-linearly with every added process, rule, or feature due to interdependencies. Place the burden of proof strictly on those advocating for added complexity, and routinely eliminate non-essential structures—such as removing formal job titles entirely.
- **Saturate employee capacity:** Assign significantly more high-value work to talented individuals than feels comfortable. This forces surgical prioritization, eliminates rigid task-list mentalities, and builds psychological comfort with managing immense possibilities.

### **4. Drive Operational Excellence via Shared Systems**

- **Build a central technological operating system:** Centralise core functions (e.g., payment infrastructure, AB testing, lifetime value modeling, AI orchestration) onto a unified, proprietary software platform. Shared infrastructure allows new business units to scale without duplicating operational headcount.
- **Empower small, elite teams:** Small teams with high talent density operating without red tape consistently out-innovate and out-produce large, bureaucratic organizations.
- **Focus strictly on ROI-positive work:** Most elective work in companies is low-return or ROI-negative. Winning requires ruthlessly prioritizing the narrow subset of projects that yield exceptionally high returns.

### **5. Apply Rational Logic Over Rigid Metrics**

- **Prioritise logic over raw data adherence:** Mechanistic KPIs and variable-pay incentives often create short-term distortions that harm holistic value creation. Numbers are only approximations of reality and must be evaluated through logic, context, and long-term rational analysis.
- **Delay major strategic decisions deliberately:** Postpone high-stakes, reversible decisions as long as rationally possible to gather maximum information and adapt to shifting conditions.
