**ISO/IEC 27001:2022** is an international standard that specifies the requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an **Information Security Management System (ISMS)** within the context of an organization. Its primary goal is to preserve the **confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information** by applying a structured risk management process, which gives confidence to interested parties that security risks are adequately managed. The standard is generic, meaning its requirements are designed to apply to any organization, regardless of its type, size, or nature.

### Core Clauses of the Standard (Clauses 4–10)

The standard is organized into several key clauses that align with a high-level structure common to other ISO management standards. To claim conformity, an organization must satisfy all requirements within these clauses:

- **Context of the Organization (Clause 4):** The organization must identify the internal and external issues that affect its security goals, understand the requirements of interested parties (such as legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations), and explicitly define the scope of its ISMS.
- **Leadership (Clause 5):** Top management must demonstrate active leadership by establishing an information security policy, setting compatible objectives, providing necessary resources, and assigning roles, responsibilities, and authorities.
- **Planning (Clause 6):** Organizations must define a repeatable risk assessment process to identify security risks, assess their potential consequences and likelihood, formulate a risk treatment plan, and establish measurable security objectives.
- **Support (Clause 7):** Requires ensuring that individuals doing work under the organization's control are competent and security-aware, establishing internal and external communication protocols, and maintaining controlled, documented information.
- **Operation (Clause 8):** Outlines the execution, control, and documentation of the operational processes, planned changes, and risk treatment plans established in earlier stages.
- **Performance Evaluation (Clause 9):** Mandates regular monitoring, measurement, analysis, and evaluation of the ISMS, conducting independent internal audits at planned intervals, and holding management reviews.
- **Improvement (Clause 10):** Details how organizations must react to nonconformities, take corrective actions to prevent recurrence, and drive the continual improvement of the ISMS.

### Annex A: Information Security Controls Reference

A critical part of the standard is **Annex A**, a normative reference containing a list of **information security controls** directly aligned with ISO/IEC 27002:2022. When formulating their risk treatment plans, organizations must compare their chosen controls against Annex A to ensure no necessary measures have been overlooked.

The controls are divided into **four main categories**:

1.  **Organizational Controls (5.1 to 5.37):** Deals with high-level operational policies, including asset inventories, threat intelligence, supplier relationship security, cloud services usage, and incident management.
2.  **People Controls (6.1 to 6.8):** Focuses on human resources security, including candidate screening, employment terms, remote working guidelines, and security awareness and training.
3.  **Physical Controls (7.1 to 7.14):** Governs physical security perimeters, entry controls, equipment protection, clear desk/clear screen rules, and securing facilities against physical and environmental threats.
4.  **Technological Controls (8.1 to 8.34):** Addresses technical measures, such as access control restrictions, secure authentication, malware protection, logging, network security, configuration management, cryptography, and secure development life cycle principles.
